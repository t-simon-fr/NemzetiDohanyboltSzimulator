# #122 – Implementation plan

Authorized runtime/UI scope: exactly stock/choice, cleanliness, prices, service time; daily bounded reputation, TobaccOS overview/summary and modest arrival/basket/regular effects. No new characters, save system, direct cash reward or Pages release.

Measure service from physical entry to successful settlement or dismissal, including queue and payment; use an Open-only reputation clock (pause/help, preparation and post-shift do not age observations; in-world monitor/terminal focus DOES count so payment time cannot be hidden). Coffee/lottery get larger allowances. Missing stock/alternative events belong only to choice; price refusals only to price; other dismissals count as poor service, so skipping cannot improve averages. Thieves are excluded. Sample available unlocked kinds before delivery processing; pending stock never counts and duplicate shelves never add kinds. Coffee and owned lottery use their real available supplies. Tickets and ice cream stay orderable from day one, so they join the choice denominator only once the player actually carries them (ordered this game, waiting in the warehouse or standing on a shelf), and a carried category keeps counting after it sells out; a starter shop that only sells cigarettes therefore keeps full choice instead of losing it to categories it never offered. Dirt ages accumulate only during open service and persist across days until cleaned.

Configuration in one pure module: initial50, weights30/20/20/30, minimum30 observed seconds and3 completed visits, confidence120 seconds/3 service observations, smoothing18%, max daily change4. Mean ratings capped by the weakest observed factor +35 to prevent excellent cleaning masking uniformly rejected sales. No evidence keeps neutral values and does not change reputation. Price rating uses the worse of price/reference and observed refusal rate, not two additive penalties. Choice similarly uses the worse of actual availability and missing-order rate.

At neutral50 preserve existing basket pattern and12–28s arrival range. Arrival multiplier bounded0.8–1.2; basket sizes1–3 with at most20% one-step change; regular returns at most45% above neutral from previously served existing people and actually available favourites. Seeded pure samples keep comparisons repeatable; lottery owner/claim selection and forced debug visits take precedence. Contracts/new SKU unlock rules remain authoritative.

Affected: new pure ShopReputationState/config and thin ShopReputation adapter/UI; controller tick/entry/dismiss/day, checkout completion, bootstrap, current NPC identity/dialogue, existing order size, activities spill view, computer app/shell and summary. Meaningful maintained reputation-state and reputation-integration suites plus stock-aware-orders/continuous-arrivals/lottery/partner/UI/day regressions. Targeted native checks, one stable full native profile, actual native Game-view review and one final WebGL player with real mouse/keyboard/720p/1080p flow. Verified PR merged to main, issue stages reconciled. Keep unrelated materials/project settings/recovery files intact.
