# Hungarian forint imagery

Source: **Magyar Nemzeti Bank (MNB)**, https://www.mnb.hu/ . Banknote graphic designs: **Pálinkás György** (renewed series).

The six renewed banknotes and six ordinary circulating coins are official MNB illustrations. The original downloaded JPEG/PNG bytes, including existing **MINTA** markings, are retained unchanged in `Assets/Game/Resources/Currency/HUF`. Banknote front/back regions are selected through mesh/material UVs; coin faces use circular geometry. Images are not repainted or used to imply MNB endorsement. The in-game shift guide (H) includes the source credit.

[forint-sources.json](forint-sources.json) records every original URL, denomination, side, retrieval date and SHA-256. Banknotes use the upper/lower face in the original composite. Coin numeric reverse faces point upward; motif obverses are on the underside.
