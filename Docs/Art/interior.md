# Starter shop interior art

The first environment art pass focuses on the counter and cigarette wall. It retains the user's counter placement, physical register, computer, delivery tray and customer routes. The room remains a modest Hungarian corner shop with warm painted plaster, ceramic floor, walnut laminate, charcoal counter surface and dark teal metalwork.

## Assets and editing

- Generated surface textures: `Assets/Game/Art/Interior/FloorTiles.png`, `Walnut.png`, `WallPaint.png`.
- Unity materials and surface meshes: `Assets/Game/Art/Interior/`.
- Apply/regenerate in Edit mode: **Nemzeti Dohánybolt > Apply Interior Art** (`Assets/Game/Editor/InteriorArtSetup.cs`).
- Main scene: `Assets/Game/Scenes/ShopPrototype.unity`.
- Before/after views: `Builds/InteriorPreview/before-shop.png`, `final-shop.png`, `final-rack.png`, `final-computer.png`.
- Verification report: `Builds/InteriorPreview/checks.json`.

Floor UVs use a 1.2 metre texture repeat with four tiles per texture edge; wall and wood meshes use scale-aware planar UVs. The counter retains its original transform and interaction collider, with a beveled visual top, front panel joints, kick plate and narrow brass trim. Coffee mug, plant and credit notice are attached to the counter assembly and follow movement/rotation.

The existing 32 stock positions remain eight per brand, with 25 cm horizontal spacing and 35 cm shelf spacing. The cigarette cabinet is 2.2 metres wide, with side panels, front ticket rails, small prices on every shelf, lane dividers and four brand labels. There are no fake decorative cigarette packs. Capacity, stock, customer orders and pickup remain driven by the original product objects.

Managed decoration roots are `Interior Art`, `Interior Counter Detail`, `Interior Countertop Props`, `Interior Rack Detail` and each wall's `Interior Wall Trim`. They contain no colliders, so decoration does not intercept use rays or block the player. The expansion partition owns its trim; both disappear together when expanding the shop. Reapplying the art pass replaces only managed roots and keeps the object count stable. The packaging upgrader reapplies the dense rack layout when art is present; rebuilding the compact shop applies the art pass after packaging.

Lighting uses two modeled fluorescent fixtures, downward spot fills, a small cigarette display fill and trilight ambient. The directional fill has real-time shadows disabled to avoid visible ceiling-edge shadow-map artifacts; this pass does not bake lightmaps. Existing warehouse lighting remains. Practical fixtures use an emissive diffuser material. All lighting uses URP-compatible point/spot/directional sources. Added details include a small opening-hours card, 18+ badge, coffee, plant, credit notice and 'A bajusz nem okmány' reminder.

## Texture generation provenance

Mode: built-in image_gen tool. Three separate tileable albedo textures were generated, inspected and copied into the project. Geometry, UV mapping, text signs, fixtures and props are editable Unity assets generated by the editor script. Runtime assets do not reference the generation cache. Exact final prompts are recorded in `Docs/Art/interior-texture-prompts.txt`.
